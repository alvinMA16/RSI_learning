Abstract
Letkhave characteristic zero and letf∈k[x 0, x1, x2, x3] be homogeneous of degree
five with det Hess(f) = 0 and generic (function-field) Hessian rank three. We prove
that the Hessian kernel contains a nonzeroconstantvectore∈k 4 – equivalently,f
is a cone: after a linear change of coordinates it depends on three variables. This is
the quintic, corank-one case of the classical Gordan–Noether theorem that quaternary
forms with vanishing Hessian are cones; the proof given here is new, elementary, and
effective. It runs entirely inside the polynomial ring: the unique polar relation of the
gradient produces a primitive self-vanishing kernel field by a locally-nilpotent-derivation
gcd descent; a degeneration-of-trajectories argument forces the projective image of that
field to be a single line; and a first-order Wronskian elimination then forces the field to
be constant or the rank to drop. Every algebraic identity in the proof is certified by an
exact frozen checker.
The homogeneity hypothesis is not removable. We exhibit the explicit inhomoge-
neous potential
f=x(z 2 +z 4) +y(z 3 +z 5) +w 3
with layers of degrees 3,4,5,6, whose Hessian has generic rank three and whose one-
dimensional kernel field ismoving: no nonzero constant kernel vector exists. The
example is sharp in a precise sense: the polar relation, the primitive descent, and the
self-vanishing identity all survive verbatim, and exactly the two consequences of Euler’s
identity fail. A short independent argument – found by an adversarial audit inside the
source campaign – isolates the same mechanism on the subclass of quintics linear in two
variables, where Euler’s relation upgrades proportional gradients to a constant ratio in
one line.
The theorem removes the last literature dependency from the homogeneous 3+4+5
stratification of an ongoing program on the four-variable Hessian conjecture HC(4),
whose resolution would decide the planar Jacobian conjecture; that program remains
open and nothing beyond the stated theorems is claimed here.

Motivation
The question is not antiquarian. Writing HC(4) for the four-variable Hessian (Legendre-
transform) conjecture, one has the implication HC(4)⇒JC(2) – the planar Jacobian
conjecture – via the symmetric reduction of de Bondt and van den Essen [2]; following
the 2026 collapse of the higher-dimensional Jacobian landscape (an explicit non-injective
Keller map onC 3, and Meng–Yang’s five-variable refutation of the Hessian conjecture,
leaving exactlyn= 4 open [6]), the four-variable case became the live gateway. The
source campaign stratifies Keller potentials with layers 3+4+5; every stratum theorem
needed one upstream premise, namely aconstantmissing direction for the quintic layer.
Under the campaign’s standing rule that no literature theorem may sit inside a proof chain,
Theorem 1.1 supplies that premise in-house – it was, at campaign close, the most heavily
audited single claim in the fleet ledger – and Theorem 1.2 shows the premise is genuinely
about the homogeneous layer: it fails for the inhomogeneous total potential. HC(4) itself,
and hence JC(2), remain open; nothing in this paper claims otherwise.

6 Effectivity and machine verification
The proof of Theorem 1.1 is effective: givenf, the polar relationGis computable by
elimination, the kernel fieldvby differentiation and gcd removal, and the final constant
direction by linear algebra. Every displayed identity is certified by the frozen exact checker
check hc4 three layer rank3 top kernel constancy.py: the transported cubic coeffi-
cient (6), all four Wronskian eliminations (11), a rank-three constant-direction control, and a
negative control (imposing the moving fieldv= (0,0, r, p) on a generic homogeneous quintic
leaves exactly the six binary quintics in (r, p), all of Hessian rank at most two). A separate
exact search over coprime moving fields of degrees one to three returned zero non-binary
survivors (data/hc4 three layer rank3 moving top kernel search.json); this search is
falsification evidence only – the theorem rests on Section 3. Theorem 1.2 is certified by
check hc4 weighted multilayer rank3 moving kernel.py. In the source ledger the two
results underwent two hostile referee rounds each and carry six recorded cross-window audit
verdicts by five distinct auditors, allconfirmed; one auditor’s verdict isconfirmed-with-
named-scope, its content being Proposition 5.1 together with the explicit statement that
the general (nonlinear in (x, y)) case was confirmed only against attack, not re-derived –
a scope the present paper’s Section 3 closes with the full argument. No step has been
formalized in a proof assistant.
